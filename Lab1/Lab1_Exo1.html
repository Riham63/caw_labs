Brief History

The history of Palestine spans thousands of years, with civilizations such as the Canaanites, Hebrews, and Romans leaving their marks. Throughout history, the land has been at the crossroads of cultures and empires.

In the 20th century, Palestine became the center of the Arab-Israeli conflict, especially after the creation of Israel in 1948, which led to displacement and ongoing struggles for statehood and rights.

Despite many challenges, the Palestinian people continue to fight for independence, recognition, and the right to live in peace and dignity on their ancestral land.
