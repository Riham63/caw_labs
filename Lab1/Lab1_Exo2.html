Since October 7, 2023, the Gaza Strip has faced a catastrophic humanitarian crisis. According to United Nations and health ministry reports, more than 41,000 Palestinians have been killed, including over 16,000 children and 10,000 women. Tens of thousands more have been injured or are still missing under the rubble.

The destruction of infrastructure is unprecedented: about 70% of Gaza’s buildings have been damaged or destroyed, including homes, hospitals, schools, mosques, and communication networks. More than 1.9 million people — nearly 85% of the population — have been displaced.

The siege has completely cut off food, fuel, and medical supplies. Hospitals no longer function normally, and access to clean water and electricity is almost nonexistent. Gaza is now on the brink of famine and disease.
