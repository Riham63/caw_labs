Artificial Intelligence in Daily Life

What is AI?

AI means giving machines the ability to think and learn like humans. It helps computers solve problems and make smart decisions.

How AI Helps Us

Artificial Intelligence (AI) allows machines to process information, recognize patterns, and make decisions without direct human control. It is used in many fields such as medicine, education, and transportation. For example, AI can help doctors detect diseases earlier, make learning more personalized for students, and improve the safety of self-driving cars. As AI continues to develop, it is changing how we live and work every day.
